## Notes before applying choices

- “Tallest” will be decided by verified physical height, not by visual preference. Where the table shows competing or unverified candidates, the choice indicates which association boundary to research rather than overriding the measurements.
- A metropolitan landmark outside the municipality is accepted only with documented city association.
- Observation rides, wheels, guyed masts, demolished landmarks, and reconstructions that do not meet the permanent-landmark rule remain excluded.
